FAQ(Frequently Asked Questions)

1. Who can apply in this portal(https://waterallocationod.nic.in)

Ans: Following 19 nos of commercial and other establishment can apply for allocation in this portal. Application for new case, reduction, enhancement, phasing, re-phasing & regularization of allocation can be availed.

  1. Diary Farm
  2. Paultry Farm
  3. Hatchery
  4. Country Liquor
  5. Construction Of Road
  6. Construction Of Building
  7. Fishery
  8. Port
  9. Railway
  10. Airport
  11. Hospital
  12. Garage
  13. Institutions (IIT, NIGER, Engineering College, University etc.)
  14. Offices (Government, Private)
  15. PHD
  16. RWS&S
  17. IDCO (SPV)
  18. Marketing Mall
  19. Show Room
2. If i am not coming in the above 19 categories, where can i apply ?

Ans: Other category of cases can be applied through GO-SWIFT portal(https://investodisha.gov.in) developed by industries Dept.

3. Which documents shall be uploaded in the portal for surface water allocation ?

Ans: The following documents to be uploaded

(1) Mandatory Documents:

  • Intake location on colored topo shet indicating Lat; & Long;
  • Industry location on colored topo shet indicating Lat; & Long;
  • Assessment certificate from competent authority.
  • Note on calculation of quantity indented.

(2) Other Documents:

  • MOU signed with Government / single window approval receipt.
  • Recommandation letter
  • Layout of drawal mechanism
  • Recycling proposal
  • Waste water generation proposal
  • Quntity of effluent / method of disposal
  • Lean period water management plan
  • Brief note on activities of the organization
4. Whcich document shall be uploaded in the portal for ground water allocation ?

Ans: In addition to the above documents at Sl no.3 NOC from CGWA to be uploaded.

Wheather security deposit & Processinf fee shall be paid online at one go or made separately ?

Ans: Portal has been designed to calculate processing fee & security deposit as per the quantity indented and will direct to pay both amount in one go.

How many queries can be raised by the department?

Ans: Maximum 2 number of queries can be raised tor insufficient documents/information, noncompliance of which shall lead to rejection of application.

If any difficulty in system arises during filling up the application, which office is to be contacted ?

Ans: For any system difficulty arises in filling up the application, NIC Bhubaneswar State Center may be contacted.
